# Show and podcast artwork

These fifteen covers match the owner's existing lists, in the same order. They were
visually verified on September 21, 2026. Local WebP copies are proportionally reduced
without upscaling or cropping: at most 360 × 540 pixels for shows, 360 × 360 for podcasts.
Show cells are 2:3 and podcast cells are square; object-fit: contain retains the full artwork.
All use lazy loading, native title labels, descriptive image alt text, and the existing
16px grid gaps and muted red hover outline. They do not add external links.
These source records document provenance and do not assert image licenses.
