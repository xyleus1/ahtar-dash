# Movie poster sources

Posters were matched to the owner's film list and visually checked on September 21, 2026.
Display copies are local WebP images, proportionally reduced to at most 360 × 540 pixels,
without upscaling or additional cropping. The 2:3 grid uses object-fit: contain to preserve
the entire poster. Images load lazily and use the film title as their accessible name.
These source links record provenance; they are not license claims.

The Passenger is the upcoming Magnus von Horn film with Jeremy Strong, as identified by
[FilmNation's announcement](https://filmnation.com/about/media-coverage/jeremy-strong-starring-in-escape-thriller-the-passenger-from-director-magnus-von-horn-filmnation-la).
No matching poster was found. Letterboxd and Filmow only provided generic placeholders.
Its place in the grid retains a plain title, without substituting a different film.

Dekalog uses the artwork for the complete 1989 series. End of Summer uses the 2014
Jóhann Jóhannsson film. Polia & Blastema is identified as 2021 in
[the official L'Étrange Festival program](https://etrangefestival.com/2023/en/show/the-begotten-cycle);
Letterboxd labels the same film 2022.
